    Your saved insight
    How super fits your freedom plan
    Most Australians have two pots of money growing at the same time, and most calculators ignore one of them.

    Pot 1: your savings. Everything outside super: ETFs, cash, offset, shares. You can get to this any time. It's what FirePath uses to work out your freedom date.

    Pot 2: your super. Growing in the background, with your employer adding to it every pay. You can't get to it until 60, but it's growing the whole time.

    So the question isn't only whether your savings can hit your freedom number on their own. It's how the two fit together, and what gap (if any) you need to close yourself.

    Super projected at 7% annual growth with employer contributions after 15% contributions tax. Withdrawals after age 60 are generally tax-free. Educational only — not financial advice.
