How long should it last?

Stopping work before 50? Your money may need to last 40 years or more.

2.5%

The 4% rule comes from research showing a 4% annual withdrawal lasted 30 years in most past periods for a spread of investments. Most, not all. For a longer retirement, many people use 3–3.5%.

Markets don't return the average every year. A bad few years right after you stop work hurt most, because you're selling while prices are down (this is called sequence risk). So this tests your plan against 2,000 different orders of good and bad years.

What if I spend more?

Same savings, different withdrawal rates. A bigger income now means less room for bad years.

Your takeaway

These numbers assume your investments grow at the rate you've chosen, with capital gains, dividends and everything combined. Actual returns vary and depend on what you're invested in.
