"I'm a support worker, and I created FirePath because I wish I had this knowledge in simple terms in my early 20s."

Over the years, I've talked to heaps of people about life, stress and making ends meet. One thing keeps coming up: most people don't quite get how money works. Not because they're not smart — nobody ever explained it to them.

My wife and I took some calculated risks to move our family forward. They felt uncomfortable at the time, but we wouldn't be where we are without them. I wish we'd made them a decade earlier — the more time you have, the easier it is to get ahead.

How it works

Three things FirePath does differently.

It shows you. It educates you. It empowers you.

1
Shows you where you actually stand
Most of us have a rough idea of our finances, but rough ideas don't give you a plan. FirePath turns confusing financial information into something you can actually understand — including the date you could become financially independent, and what to do about it if that date doesn't fit your life.
2
Tells you what actually moves the needle
Not generic tips — your numbers. What happens to your date if you save $50 more a week, or invest instead of leaving it in a bank? FirePath shows the exact difference, and the Learning Lab recommends education built around your situation, so you know what to do next and why it matters.
3
Sits beside you as your situation changes
Life changes. Your numbers should too. Every time you come back, FirePath updates your picture. Over time you'll watch the most important number in your life get smaller — and that feeling is what we want you to experience.
