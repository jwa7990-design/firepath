# Suggested additions to the Terms of Service: draft for legal review

**Status:** items 1 (not a registered tax agent) and 2 (AI explanations) were added to the Terms (src/pages/terms.astro) in October 2026 at the owner's request, word for word as below. Items 3–5 are still draft only. A lawyer's review of the final wording is still recommended.

**Why:** an adviser-eyes review of FirePath (October 2026) found the Terms already say FirePath gives general information, isn't financial, investment or tax advice, and holds no AFSL. It also found four gaps an Australian licensed adviser or compliance lawyer would expect to see covered. The wording below is a starting point for a lawyer, not legal advice.

---

## 1. Not a registered tax agent

*Suggested place: "Not financial advice" section, after the first paragraph.*

> FirePath isn't a registered tax agent or BAS agent. Tax figures on FirePath (including take-home pay, salary sacrifice, HELP, offset accounts, Division 293, franking and capital gains) are general estimates based on the rules as we understand them and the numbers you enter. They aren't tax advice and may not reflect your circumstances. For advice on your own tax, speak to a registered tax agent or the ATO.

*Reason: several Pro tools and the free calculator show tax amounts. The Tax Agent Services Act covers tax advice about a person's own circumstances.*

## 2. AI-written content

*Suggested place: new short section after "Not financial advice", titled "AI explanations".*

> Some parts of FirePath Pro use artificial intelligence to explain your numbers in plain English, including Ask FirePath and AI explanations on your dashboard. AI-written text is general information only. It can be wrong or incomplete, it doesn't know your full situation, and it isn't a recommendation to do anything. Please check anything important against the figures shown and, for decisions about your money, speak to a licensed financial adviser.

*Reason: the Terms mention "AI insights" once, but nothing describes what the AI is, its limits, or that it can make mistakes.*

## 3. Standard general advice warning wording

*Suggested place: the "Important" notice at the top, or the start of "Not financial advice".*

> The information on FirePath is general in nature and doesn't take into account your objectives, financial situation or needs. Before acting on any information, consider whether it's appropriate for you, having regard to your objectives, financial situation and needs.

*Reason: this is the familiar wording Australian readers and regulators recognise for general information. The current Terms say something similar in plain English, but not in these terms.*

## 4. Product disclosure statements

*Suggested place: "Not financial advice" section.*

> FirePath doesn't recommend any financial product. If you're considering a financial product, read its product disclosure statement (PDS) and target market determination (TMD) before deciding.

*Reason: guides explain classes of products (index funds, ETFs, bonds, super). A PDS line is standard wherever products are discussed.*

## 5. Use of FirePath in decisions (consistency)

*Current: "not as the basis for any financial decision".*

The Features page used to say "See what a big decision does to your date… before you make it". That's now been changed to "One input to weigh up, alongside advice if you need it", so the marketing and the Terms say the same thing. If a lawyer prefers to soften the Terms instead, the matching wording could be:

> FirePath can help you explore how different choices might affect your numbers. It's one input to consider, not a substitute for advice about your circumstances.
